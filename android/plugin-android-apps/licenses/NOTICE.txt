Halone's built-in Android apps include:

LibADB Android 3.1.1, Copyright 2021 Muntashir Al-Islam.
https://github.com/MuntashirAkon/libadb-android/tree/3.1.1
Used under the Apache License 2.0 (upstream also offers GPL 3); see libadb/.
Source in src/main/java/io/github/muntashirakon/adb, with the local changes
made by The Unbound Launcher (github.com/MarkPronkin/the-unbound-launcher,
MIT): bounded TCP/read timeouts, null-safe pairing cleanup, draining queued
bytes when a short shell command closes its stream, bounded stream-open waits.

The on-device pairing and helper start-up follow The Unbound Launcher's
embedded ADB (MIT, Copyright 2026 The Unbound Launcher contributors).

SPAKE2 Android 2.2.1, Copyright 2021 Muntashir Al-Islam.
https://github.com/MuntashirAkon/spake2-java/tree/2.2.1
GNU Lesser General Public License version 3; see spake2/. Its Java classes
are in src/main/java/io/github/muntashirakon/crypto/spake2, and its native
library (src/main/jniLibs/*/libspake2.so) is the one in the published
spake2-android 2.2.1 AAR (SHA-256
8798fb6c04b5d53a6307ed9481e9afe563227abd4e8b9e917c8514fa850071bb),
unmodified, so it can be replaced with one built from that source.

Conscrypt Android 2.5.3 (Apache-2.0) and Bouncy Castle bcprov-jdk15to18 1.81
(MIT-style) are linked from Maven Central under their own licenses.
